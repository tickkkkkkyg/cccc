frequently asked questions

Infrastructure FAQs

What CPUs does XyleHosting use?

XyleHosting uses AMD Ryzen 9 processors for premium game hosting plans and Intel Xeon Gold 5412U for standard Minecraft plans. Both deliver excellent single-thread performance for game servers — critical for smooth gameplay.

Does XyleHosting include DDoS protection on all plans?

Yes — all plans include automatic always-on DDoS protection powered by NeoProtect at no extra charge. Attacks are detected and scrubbed in under 5 seconds, keeping your server online regardless of attack size.

How fast is XyleHosting's storage?

All servers use enterprise NVMe SSDs with sequential read speeds up to 7,000 MB/s — approximately 12 times faster than standard SATA SSDs. This means instant world loading for Minecraft and near-zero disk latency for databases.

Where are XyleHosting's servers located?

We have servers in Nepal, India (Mumbai), Singapore, and Germany (Frankfurt). See the full details on our Data Centers page.

What RAM does XyleHosting use?

We use DDR5 ECC registered memory on all nodes, providing both high bandwidth and error correction for maximum stability. ECC RAM prevents the silent data corruption that can occur under heavy load — critical for production servers.
