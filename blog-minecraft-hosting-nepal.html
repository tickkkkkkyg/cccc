Why Location Matters for Minecraft Hosting

Minecraft is highly latency-sensitive. Every player action — placing blocks, opening inventories, fighting mobs — is sent to the server and returned as an update. If your server is in Germany and your players are in Nepal, that round-trip adds 150–200ms of baseline latency before any other lag sources even come into play.

XyleHosting operates servers in Nepal, India (Mumbai), Singapore, and Germany (Frankfurt). For Nepali players, our Nepal and India nodes deliver pings as low as 5–15ms — making your server feel as responsive as a local LAN game.

Key insight: Choose a Minecraft hosting provider with servers in South Asia for the best player experience in Nepal. A low-price server with high ping is worse than a slightly pricier local server.

What to Look for in Minecraft Hosting

1. CPU Single-Core Performance

Minecraft's game logic runs predominantly on a single CPU thread (the main server tick). This means raw GHz matters more than core count for most servers. A provider using an AMD Ryzen 9 at 5.1 GHz boost will give you noticeably smoother TPS (ticks per second) than a budget host running outdated server-grade CPUs at 2.5 GHz — even if the latter has more cores. Our Minecraft Ryzen plans specifically use AMD Ryzen 9 processors for this reason.

2. RAM Allocation

As a general guide for vanilla Minecraft in 2026:

  • 2 GB RAM — suitable for 1–20 players, vanilla or lightly-modded
  • 4 GB RAM — recommended for 20–50 players or small modpacks (e.g. FTB Lite)
  • 8 GB RAM — ideal for 50–100 players or heavy modpacks (RLCraft, ATM9)
  • 16 GB+ RAM — for 100+ players or large-scale networks with BungeeCord

3. NVMe SSD Storage

World chunks in Minecraft are constantly read and written to disk. Slow SATA HDDs cause chunk loading lag — that familiar "popping" effect as you move through your world. NVMe SSDs eliminate this completely. All XyleHosting plans include NVMe SSD storage, a feature some providers charge extra for. See our full hardware specifications.

4. DDoS Protection

Popular Minecraft servers are frequent DDoS targets — especially servers running competitive minigames or large communities. Without DDoS protection, a single cheap attack can take your server offline for hours. XyleHosting includes automatic NeoProtect DDoS mitigation on every plan, with up to 1 Tbps+ scrubbing capacity and sub-5-second response time.
