common questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does it take to set up a server?

Most servers are provisioned and ready within 2–5 minutes of payment confirmation. Minecraft servers are available instantly. VPS servers may take up to 5 minutes for OS installation.

Do I need technical experience?

No. Minecraft and web hosting require zero technical knowledge — everything is managed through a simple control panel. For VPS, basic Linux knowledge is helpful but our support team is always available to assist.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit/debit cards, PayPal, eSewa, and Khalti for customers in Nepal. All payments are processed securely through billing.xyle.host.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. You can upgrade any plan at any time from the billing panel. Upgrades are applied without data loss and take effect immediately.

What is the refund policy?

XyleHosting offers a 72-hour money-back guarantee on new orders. Contact support within the first 72 hours for a full refund. See our Refund Policy for full details.

Which server location is best for Nepal players?

Our Nepal and India data centers offer the lowest latency for South Asian players. Germany is ideal for European users, and Singapore for Southeast Asia.
